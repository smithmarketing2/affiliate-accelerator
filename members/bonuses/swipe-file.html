🎯 Headline Formula

List

10 [Topic] Tips That Actually Work in 2025

10 Affiliate Marketing Tips That Actually Work in 2025

Great for blog posts and Pinterest pins. Replace bracket with your topic.

How-To

How to [Result] Without [Objection]

How to Make Your First $1,000 Without a Website

Removes barriers and objections. High click-through rate.

Mistake

[Number] [Topic] Mistakes You're Making Right Now

Creates urgency and curiosity. People fear making mistakes.

Beginner

The Beginner's Guide to [Topic] (Step-by-Step)

Attracts beginners who want comprehensive guidance.

Secret

Targets people researching before buying. High buyer intent.
