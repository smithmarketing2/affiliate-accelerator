📌 Pinterest Traffic Playbook

Step-by-step system to drive thousands of free visitors from Pinterest

0% Complete — Check off items as you finish them
1

Account Setup & Optimization

Days 1-3: Foundation for success

  • Create Business Account — Convert personal to business or create new at business.pinterest.com
  • Claim Your Website — Add meta tag or upload HTML file for verification
  • Optimize Profile — Keyword-rich name, clear description, branded profile photo
  • Create 5-10 Boards — Each focused on specific keywords in your niche
  • Enable Rich Pins — Apply at developers.pinterest.com/docs/rich-pins/

💡 Pro Tip

Use your target keywords in your profile name (e.g., "Sarah | Affiliate Marketing Tips" instead of just "Sarah")

2

Keyword Research & Strategy

Days 4-5: Find what people are searching for

  • Use Pinterest Search — Type your niche and note autocomplete suggestions
  • Analyze Competitors — Find top accounts in your niche, see what pins perform best
  • Create Keyword List — Build spreadsheet of 50+ keywords organized by topic
  • Check Trends — Use trends.pinterest.com to find rising searches
  • Seasonal Planning — Map content to seasonal trends (holidays, events, seasons)
3

Pin Creation System

Days 6-10: Create pins that get clicks

  • Set Up Canva — Create 1000x1500px template with your branding
  • Title Formula — Use: Number + Adjective + Keyword + Promise (e.g., "10 Easy Affiliate Marketing Tips for Beginners")
  • Create 30 Pins — Batch create for the month using templates
  • Video Pins — Create 5 short video pins (Idea Pins) for extra reach
  • Mobile Optimization — Test all pins on mobile; 85% of Pinterest users are mobile

💡 Pro Tip

Pins with text overlay get 23% more engagement. Keep text to top 30% of image for best results.

4

Scheduling & Automation

Days 11-14: Set up consistent posting

  • Choose Scheduler — Tailwind (recommended), Later, or Pinterest's native scheduler
  • Create Posting Schedule — 5-15 pins per day (mix of fresh and repins)
  • Schedule 2 Weeks Ahead — Batch schedule to stay consistent
  • Set Up Tailwind Communities — Join 5-10 relevant tribes for reciprocal pinning
  • Pin to Multiple Boards — Schedule each pin to 3-5 relevant boards over time
5

Traffic Optimization

Ongoing: Scale what works

  • Track Analytics Weekly — Check Pinterest Analytics for top performers
  • Double Down on Winners — Create similar pins to your top 10%
  • Test New Formats — Try carousel pins, video pins, and collections
  • Optimize Underperformers — Refresh titles/descriptions on low-performing pins
  • Build Email List — Add lead magnets to your landing pages for Pinterest traffic

💡 Pro Tip

Pinterest is a search engine, not social media. Focus on SEO keywords, not followers. Pins can drive traffic for months or years!
